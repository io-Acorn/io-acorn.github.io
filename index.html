Acorn 8.5.1

Acorn is a bitmap image editor designed for simplicity. Fast, fluid, and intuitive, it delivers essential tools without excess. Layer-based editing meets GPU acceleration for seamless performance on macOS.

Effortless Screenshots

Capture screenshots directly in Acorn and edit instantly. No switching apps—refine, filter, and export in one fluid workflow.

Layer-Based Power

Industry-standard layers with opacity, blending modes, vector shapes, and text. Control every detail with precision.

Filter Chains & Effects

Chain filters for stunning results. Gradients, selections (freeform, elliptical, magic wand), and custom text styles at your fingertips.

Adaptive Canvas

Resize images and canvas by dragging the window. Full-screen mode utilizes every pixel; tablet support handles pressure and eraser input.

Extensible & Accelerated

GPU-powered for speed. Extend with Python or Objective-C plugins. New in 8.5.1: Data Merge export and enhanced mask sampling.

How It Works

1. Capture or Import

Screenshot with Acorn, snap via iSight, or open existing images. Tabs keep multiple projects accessible.

2. Layer & Edit

Build with layers, shapes, text. Apply filters, selections, and adjustments. GPU handles heavy lifts smoothly.

3. Refine & Export

Tweak with blending, opacity, or new Data Merge. Resize on the fly and export—ready for anywhere.
